    Module Detail

    Meeting Point, Meeting Hub

    Complete Meeting Lifecycle

    Meeting Point manages the full meeting lifecycle, from preparation through execution to follow-up, ensuring every meeting produces actionable output with proper accountability.

    Before Meeting

    • Quickly view and schedule meetings
    • Define meeting agenda and topics
    • Set clear goals for teams to drive better results
    • Invite team and attendees with access levels
    • Attach presentations and documents
    • Set allowed time per presenter

    During Meeting

    • Capture MOM in real-time (notes, tasks, decisions)
    • Voting on recommendations and action items
    • Reference previous meetings and topics
    • Timer for time management
    • Move topics to another meeting (parking slot)
    • Recurring meeting support

    After Meeting

    • Draft MOM Approval Final MOM workflow
    • Tasks and actions follow-up and tracking
    • Export MOM in PDF, share with stakeholders
    • Customized MOM template with branding

    Value Proposition

    Paperless Documentation

    All meeting notes, files, and documents safely stored in one place for quick retrieval at any time.

    Better Accountability

    All meeting actions and tasks assigned real-time during the meeting with proper follow-up and reminders.

    Actionable Output

    Action items created for everyone to turn meeting insights into positive output.

    Performance Assessment

    Monitor and improve team performance by tracking all tasks and needed actions.

    Features & Functionality

    Meeting Agenda

    Define topics, presenters, attachments, and allowed time per agenda item.

    Voting

    In-meeting voting on decisions and recommendations with percentage tracking.

    MOM & Attachments

    Notes, tasks, and decisions captured during meeting with attachments support.

    Analytics & Reporting

    Meeting analytics and reporting for performance tracking and trend analysis.

    Attendees Management

    Members, supervisors, and guests with role-based access and notifications.

    Actions & Tasks

    Real-time task assignment during meeting with owner, due date, and status tracking.

    Module Detail

    Gates, Visitor Pass Management

    Digital Visitor Management

    Gates replaces paper-based visitor logs with a fully digital check-in system, enhancing security, professionalism, and operational efficiency with automated tracking and notifications.

    Core Features

    • Digital check-in/check-out with QR codes
    • Customizable branding (logo, colors, text)
    • Visitor recognition, name displayed on screen
    • Instant photo capture for badges
    • NDA and safety instruction signing on screen
    • Express check-in via QR in invitation emails
    • Multi-location management from single account
    • Smart pre-registrations with automatic invitations
    • Host notifications via SMS, email, and voice
    • Automatic end-of-day reports
    • Badge printing with custom designs
    • Integration with access control systems

    Value Proposition

    Professional Image

    Enhance enrollment process and visitor check-in with a modern digital system.

    Enhanced Security

    Identify who is in your facility quickly and accurately, especially in emergencies.

    Analytics & Reporting

    Fully Automated
