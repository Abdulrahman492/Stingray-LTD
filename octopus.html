    Core Features

    Everything You Need to Deliver

    Project Charter

    Comprehensive charter with scope definition, objectives, success criteria, assumptions, constraints, and deliverables. Linked to strategic objectives and organizational priorities.

    Benefit Realization (BRM)

    Define expected benefits, savings formulas, KPI linkage, and tracking mechanisms. Monitor benefit achievement throughout project lifecycle with automated calculations.

    Stakeholder & RACI

    Internal and external stakeholder management with RACI matrix. Define steering committee, vendors, and client contacts with role-based access and notification rules.

    KPIs & Metrics

    Define project KPIs with targets, actual tracking, scoring patterns, and trend analysis. Link KPIs to milestones and strategic objectives for alignment visibility.

    Risk & Issues

    Risk register with probability, impact scoring, mitigation plans, and owner assignment. Issue tracking with severity, escalation workflows, and resolution tracking.

    Change Requests

    Formal CR process with impact analysis, approval workflows, and status tracking. Full audit trail with before/after comparisons and stakeholder sign-off.

    Schedule & Milestones

    Activity planning with upload from MS Project Plan. Milestone tracking with baseline comparisons, dependencies, and critical path identification.

    Communication Matrix

    Define communication plans with stakeholder mapping, frequency, channels, and escalation paths. Automated reminders and notification center integration.

    Lessons Learned & Survey

    Capture lessons at any phase. Sponsor satisfaction surveys for quality feedback. Post-implementation reviews with knowledge base integration.

    Dashboards & Metrics

    Real-Time Project Intelligence

    Dynamic dashboards with built-in formulas for progress tracking, ROI calculation, and project health scoring, providing instant visibility at every level.

    Progress Formula

    Actual Project % Complete ÷ Planned Project % Complete, automatically calculated and visualized with trend indicators and baseline deviation alerts.

    ROI Formula

    Automated ROI calculation with configurable formula based on benefit realization data, costs, and savings, tracking actual vs projected returns.

    Health Index Formula

    Weighted scoring across Charter, Plan, Risk, Issues, Reports, Stakeholders, Lessons, Communication, Change, and Milestone scores, consolidated into a single health metric.

    Steering Committee Report

    Auto-generated executive summary with project status, health index, milestone progress, budget burn, risks, and issues for committee review.

    Employee Performance

    Individual and team performance tracking across projects with workload, completion rates, and quality metrics per resource.

    KPIs Hierarchy

    Strategic metrics for CXOs, program KPIs for directors, project analytics for senior managers, and task-level tracking for analysts.
