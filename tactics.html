    OKR · Balanced Scorecard · GEM 2.0

    Objectives and Key Results with both (Benefit Key Result Deliverables) and (Key Result Tasks) approaches. Cascading from strategic to individual level.

    Four perspectives, Financial, Customer, Internal Process, Learning & Growth. Strategic perspectives with objectives, indicators, and plan views.

    Government Excellence Model alignment via configurable BSC perspectives and scoring patterns, tailored for GCC public sector requirements.
