    The Challenge

    Why Existing Tools Fall Short

    Many task management systems fail to fully meet user needs, resulting in suboptimal utilization and complexity that hinders daily operations.

    No Unified Tasks Hub

    No centralized task management hub for employees to manage running tasks or projects across multiple systems or projects.

    Dashboard & Reporting

    Lack of standard and customizable reporting preventing users from having full vision, monitoring, and control over active tasks and projects.

    Dynamic Hierarchy

    Existing systems lack customizable organizational structures, crucial for aligning with specific governmental hierarchies like those in KSA.

    Integration Flexibility

    Integration challenges with on-premise systems due to stringent security and regulatory requirements. No out-of-the-box connectors.

    Performance Tracker

    Significant gap in tracking overall performance across different organizational levels, essential for improvement and rewards.

    Lack of Customizations

    Systems don't support modifications tailored to specific needs and workflows of different teams or business units.

    Transform Task Management

    Tasky integrates machine learning, natural language processing, and predictive analytics to automate, optimize, and elevate every aspect of task management.

    AI FEATURE 01

    Smart Task Prioritization

    AI assigns priority scores based on due dates, task importance, dependencies, and historical completion patterns. A dynamically updated "Suggested Priority List" ensures teams always focus on what matters most.

    Random ForestWeighted ScoringAuto-Update
    AI FEATURE 02

    AI-Powered Recommendations

    Suggests tasks based on past behavior patterns, pending follow-ups, and contextual relevance. If a user always checks invoices on Fridays, similar tasks surface automatically.

    Collaborative FilteringNLP ContextNeural CF
    AI FEATURE 03

    Automated Task Assignment

    AI determines the best-suited person based on workload balance, skill matching, and task complexity. Workload heatmaps ensure fair distribution with manager override capability.

    K-MeansSkill MatchingLoad Balancing
    AI FEATURE 04

    NLP-Based Task Creation

    Type natural language like "Remind me to submit the report on Friday at 3 PM", AI extracts task name, deadline, assignee, and priority automatically. Voice command integration included.

    NERSpaCy / BERTVoice Commands
    AI FEATURE 05

    AI-Powered Summarization

    AI reads long task descriptions, comment threads, and reports, condensing them into concise summaries with extracted actionable points via a one-click "Summary" button.

    BART / GPTTextRankAction Extraction

    Smart Deadlines & Predictions

    Predicts task duration based on past completion patterns, assignee efficiency, and complexity. Dynamically adjusts deadlines and alerts users when a deadline is unrealistic.
